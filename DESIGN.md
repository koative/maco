# Design System: Maco

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Store, Straight"**

Maco is a working Mac App Store for open-source software, and it executes that canon without irony: a sidebar shell, Discover shelves, lockups with Get pills, spotlight cards over real screenshots, product pages with an info strip. The world supplies type, palette and density; navigation and controls stay standard web controls. Appearance follows the system (`color-scheme: light dark`), so every colour role ships as a `light-dark()` pair and the page never offers its own toggle.

The surface is quiet and bright in light mode, graphite in dark: white or #1e1e1e content beside an opaque sidebar, near-black or near-white labels, secondary grey labels, hairline separators. Colour comes from three places only: Apple link blue for actions, eleven system colours for category tiles and icon fallbacks, and the apps' own icons and screenshots. Orange means one thing: source-available. Maco is an independent directory by Koative and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Apple; the store vocabulary is a familiar interface, never a claim.

**Key Characteristics:**

- System light and dark, every role a `light-dark()` pair; no manual theme switch.
- 260px opaque sidebar on desktop; compact top bar and four-tab bottom bar at 900px and below.
- Platform system type (SF on Apple devices), bold large titles, 13–15px UI labels.
- Horizontally paged shelves of lockups, spotlights and screenshot cards.
- Real app icons and screenshots carry the visual interest; nothing is invented.
- One signature motion: the clicked app icon lifts into the product page header.

## Colors

An Apple system palette in two appearances: neutral greys that step from canvas to sidebar to elevated tile to fill, one blue for every action, and a category spectrum that only ever appears on small glyph tiles.

### Primary

- **Action Blue** (accent-fill): filled primary buttons, the brand mark, the skip link, `accent-color` for native controls, and the 26% `::selection` tint. It is the same in both appearances; its hover is a slightly brighter blue (accent-fill-hover). Text on it is white (on-color).
- **Link Blue** (link-light / link-dark): every text link, See All, the focus ring, the caret, sidebar row glyphs and the selected tab. Brightens in dark mode for contrast.
- **Get Blue** (get-ink-light / get-ink-dark): the label on Get pills and secondary buttons. Matches Link Blue in light; a paler blue in dark so it reads on the grey fill.

### Secondary

- **Source-Available Orange** (orange-light / orange-dark): the "Source available" fine print under Get, the info-strip caption, and the license note heading. Its fill (orange-fill-light / orange-fill-dark) backs the license note panel only.

### Tertiary

- **Category Spectrum** (cat-productivity, cat-developer-tools, cat-utilities, cat-media, cat-privacy-security, cat-writing, cat-design, cat-internet, cat-education, cat-games, cat-ai): eleven system colours, one per category, used as the background of category glyph tiles and of letter-tile icon fallbacks. Same value in both appearances; glyphs and letters on them are white.
- **Spotlight Stages** (stage-iina, stage-cap, stage-rectangle, stage-maccy, stage-markedit, stage-bruno): deep, desaturated tints set per spotlight in `src/lib/collections.ts`, chosen to sit behind that app's screenshot. They are data, not interface colours; a new spotlight picks a new dark tint in the same family.

### Neutral

- **Canvas** (bg-light / bg-dark): the main content column.
- **Sidebar Grey** (bg-sidebar-light / bg-sidebar-dark): the opaque sidebar, painted down the full page height by the shell gradient.
- **Elevated Grey** (bg-elevated-light / bg-elevated-dark): category tiles, collection links, screenshot frames, the contribute band, code.
- **Bar Glass** (bg-bar-light / bg-bar-dark): translucent mobile top bar and tab bar, always with backdrop blur.
- **Fill / Fill Hover** (fill-*, fill-hover-*): Get pills, secondary buttons, pagers, tags, tile hover.
- **Field** (field-light / field-dark): search fields, selects, the segmented track.
- **Selected** (selected-light / selected-dark): sidebar row hover and current row.
- **Segment Thumb** (segment-thumb-light / segment-thumb-dark): the checked segment.
- **Label** (label-light / label-dark): primary text and headings.
- **Secondary Label** (label-2-light / label-2-dark): summaries, subtitles, counts, captions, placeholders, footer.
- **Separator** (separator-light / separator-dark): every hairline: shelf tops, lockup rows, info-strip borders, sidebar edge, footer.

### Named Rules

**The Paired Role Rule.** A colour role is a `light-dark()` pair on `:root` or it is appearance-invariant on purpose (accent fill, category spectrum, stages, white on colour). Never hard-code a single-appearance neutral in a component.

**The One Meaning Rule.** Orange means source-available and nothing else; blue means "you can act on this". Category colours never style text or controls.

## Typography

**Display Font:** system-ui (SF Pro on Apple devices), falling back through -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif.
**Body Font:** the same system stack; no web fonts are loaded.
**Label/Mono Font:** ui-monospace, SF Mono, Menlo, monospace, inside Markdown code only.

**Character:** The platform's own face, set the way the store sets it: heavy, tightly tracked titles over plain, slightly tight body text. Hierarchy comes from size and weight, never from a second family.

### Hierarchy

- **Large Title** (large-title): page titles such as Discover, Categories, Search, a category or collection name.
- **Product Title** (product-title): the app name on its product page; 24px at 900px and below.
- **Spotlight Headline** (spotlight-headline): the editorial line on a spotlight card, capped at 22ch and balanced.
- **Headline** (headline): shelf and section headings; 20px at 620px and below. About page section headings share it.
- **Band Title** (band-title): the contribute band heading.
- **Info Value** (info-value): the large secondary-label value in the product info strip; long values drop to 14px.
- **Title** (title): sub-headings in Markdown descriptions and About principles.
- **Subhead** (subhead): the product summary and the search page input.
- **Body Long** (body-long): Markdown descriptions and About prose, held to 72ch and 68ch.
- **Lockup Name** (lockup-name): app names in lockups, shot cards and the spotlight band, single line with ellipsis.
- **Body** (body): the page default.
- **Button** (button) and **Get** (get): filled and pill actions.
- **Callout** (callout): sidebar rows, shelf subtitles, text links.
- **Footnote** (footnote) and **Control** (control): two-line lockup summaries, counts, segmented labels, selects, tags.
- **Caption** (caption) and **Caption 2** (caption-2): sidebar group labels (600), info captions, figcaptions, footer; fine print under Get.
- **Info Label** (info-label): the uppercase term above each info-strip value. This is a data label in a definition list, not a heading eyebrow.
- **Tab** (tab): bottom tab bar labels.

### Named Rules

**The One Family Rule.** Everything is the system stack. Never load a display face; distinction comes from 700-weight titles with negative tracking.

**The Tabular Count Rule.** Counts and result totals use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.

## Layout

A two-column shell: a 260px sticky sidebar, one viewport tall and independently scrollable, beside a main column whose content caps at 1320px. The cap is applied as growing horizontal padding (`max(pad, (100% − 1320px) / 2)`), so every child of main shares one column edge. Main padding is 30px top and 40px bottom on desktop.

Shelves stack with 40px between them; each shelf after the first opens with an 18px-padded hairline above its header. A shelf header holds the heading and optional subtitle on the left, See All and the paired pagers on the right. Tracks are horizontal, snap to start, and use the 24px gap; they show a thin native scrollbar until the pager script runs, then hide it. Lockup shelves show three columns of three rows; spotlight shelves show two-and-a-bit cards; screenshot shelves show four cards (or a 78% / 780px max figure on product pages). Search and category results use a three-column lockup grid. The category grid auto-fills 230px-minimum tiles (280px on the Categories page). The Information list is three columns.

### Responsive behaviour

- **At or below 1180px:** lockup shelves go to two columns, spotlights to 82% width, screenshot shelves to three; lockup grids and Information to two columns; the contribute band stacks.
- **At or below 900px:** pad and gap become 20px and 16px. The sidebar disappears; a sticky 52px top bar (brand, GitHub icon) and a fixed four-tab bar (Discover, Categories, Search, About) replace it, both honouring safe-area insets. Main gains bottom padding for the tab bar. Shelf tracks bleed to the screen edge with matching scroll padding; pagers hide in favour of touch scrolling. Spotlights are 360px tall; product icons are 96px; the info strip becomes a horizontally scrolling row of 118px-minimum cells.
- **At or below 620px:** lockup grids, Information, About principles, the source list and the category grid go to one column. Product icons are 72px. The segmented control fills the row and the selects flex to share it.

## Elevation & Depth

The interface itself is flat: depth comes from tonal steps (canvas, sidebar, elevated, fill) and hairline separators. Shadows belong to the software being shown. Screenshots carry a window shadow as a CSS `drop-shadow()` filter so the shadow follows the window's own alpha, at three strengths by context. The only box shadow is the 1px lift on the checked segment. Translucency appears only where real content passes beneath: the mobile bars and the spotlight lockup band.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Spotlight window** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 14px 22px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.4))`): the screenshot on a spotlight stage.
- **Product screenshot** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 8px 16px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.2))`): screenshots in the product page shelf.
- **Shot card** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 6px 10px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.18))`): screenshots in See It in Action cards.
- **Segment thumb** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.14)`): the checked segment only.

### Named Rules

**The Shadow Belongs to the Window Rule.** Only screenshots cast shadows. Cards, tiles, buttons and bars stay flat.

**The Blur Over Content Rule.** Backdrop blur is used only where content actually scrolls or sits beneath: the mobile top and tab bars (`blur(20px) saturate(1.8)` over Bar Glass) and the spotlight band (`blur(20px) saturate(1.4)` over `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6)`, a tint dense enough to keep white text at 5.7:1 or better and the summary at 4.6:1 or better even over a white screenshot). The sticky desktop sidebar is opaque because nothing passes under it.

## Shapes

Soft, consistent rounding that scales with the object: large cards round most (card), tiles and screenshot cards next (tile, frame-lg for product screenshots), fields and sidebar rows least (frame for the search field and code blocks, field for compact fields and rows). Every action is a pill (pill); the pager is a circle. App icons keep their own silhouettes; the letter fallback uses the icon squircle approximation (icon, 22.5%), and category glyph tiles round at 24% of their size. Borders are almost absent: separation is a 1px separator hairline or a tonal fill, never an outlined box.

## Components

### Buttons

Pill-shaped, filled, quiet.
- **Shape:** full pill (pill), 36px tall, 18px side padding, 6px icon gap.
- **Primary:** Action Blue fill, white 15px/600 label; hover moves to the brighter blue. Used for Suggest an App, Get on product pages (`get-large`: 96px minimum, weight 700), and Discover on the 404 page.
- **Secondary:** grey fill with Get Blue label; hover deepens the fill. View Source, Contribution Guide, Show More, reset actions.
- **Text button:** unfilled 13px Link Blue with an inline icon (Clear Filters).
- **Hover / Focus:** background-color over 0.15s; focus is the global 2px Link Blue ring at 2px offset.

### Get Pill and Fine Print

The store's signature action. A 28px pill, 68px minimum, 16px side padding, on grey fill with a 14px/700 Get Blue label; hover deepens the fill. It always links to the project's official download page and names the app in its accessible label. Beneath it, centred fine print (caption-2, 96px max) states the license short form (`-only` dropped, `-or-later` as `+`) or "Source available" in 600-weight orange. On spotlight stages the pill turns translucent white (`get-on-stage`) with white text.

### Lockup

An app row: 64px icon, single-line name, two-line clamped summary in Secondary Label, then the Get column on the right. 12px vertical padding with a separator hairline below; the last row in each shelf column drops its hairline. Hover underlines the name only. The icon link and the Get link are separate targets.

### Shelves and Pager

A section with a hairline-topped header, heading, optional subtitle, See All link (with a screen-reader suffix naming the shelf) and two 30px circular pagers. Pagers appear only when the track overflows, disable at either end (opacity 0.35), and page by one track width, smoothly unless reduced motion is set. Hidden at 900px and below.

### Spotlight Card

A 16px-radius card on a per-app tinted stage, clamp(340px, 29vw, 430px) tall (360px on mobile). The headline sits top-left in white; the real screenshot fills the stage with `object-fit: contain` anchored to the bottom, under the Spotlight window shadow. A lockup band (48px icon, name, one-line summary at 85% white) is pinned to the bottom edge over a `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6)` tint with backdrop blur; that density keeps white text at 5.7:1 or better and the summary at 4.6:1 or better even over a white screenshot. The stage pads `18px 30px 14px` on desktop, so the band overlaps the screenshot's lower edge and the blur has real pixels beneath it; at 900px and below it pads `16px 18px 40px`, so the screenshot ends just above the band and app controls never sit behind the text. The Get pill sits over the band's right end. Hover or focus lifts the screenshot 4px over 0.5s; keyboard focus rings the whole card through `:has()`.

### Shot Card

### Category Glyph Tile

A rounded square in the category's system colour with a white Lucide glyph at 58% of its size, stroke 2. Sizes in use: 20px in sidebar rows, 30px in the info strip, 36px on Discover tiles, 44px on the Categories page, 56px beside a category's large title.

### Letter-Tile Icon Fallback

When an app has no icon artwork, a squircle (22.5% radius) in one of the category colours shows the app's first letter in white at 46% of the size, weight 700. The colour is picked from the slug's character sum modulo the eleven categories, so a page of fallbacks is varied but stable. It is decorative (`aria-hidden`); the name beside it carries meaning.

### Signature: Icon View Transition

Cross-document view transitions are on (`@view-transition { navigation: auto }`) only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. On click, the script names only the clicked link's `.app-icon` `app-icon`; the product page names its header icon the same, so the icon morphs from shelf to header over 0.42s on `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` while the root cross-fades in 0.22s. Under reduced motion the transition is not declared, all transitions and animations are disabled, smooth scrolling becomes instant, and the spotlight lift is removed.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** define every new neutral as a `light-dark()` pair on `:root` and check both appearances.
- **Do** keep the desktop sidebar opaque (bg-sidebar) with a hairline edge.
- **Do** use Link Blue for links and focus, Action Blue fills for primary actions, and the grey-fill Get pill for every download.
- **Do** point every Get at the project's official download and name the app in its accessible label.
- **Do** mark source-available software in orange fine print and explain the restriction on its page.
- **Do** use the app's real icon and screenshots with their credits; fall back to the letter tile when no icon exists.
- **Do** keep shadows on screenshots only, as `drop-shadow()` filters.
- **Do** keep the icon view transition behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` and make paging instant under reduced motion.
- **Do** keep the 2px Link Blue focus ring visible on every control.

### Don't:

- **Don't** put vibrancy or backdrop blur on the sticky desktop sidebar; nothing scrolls beneath it.
- **Don't** invent ratings, reviews, star counts, download counts, prices or compatibility badges; the store shows only verified facts.
- **Don't** add eyebrows or kickers above headings.
- **Don't** imply Apple affiliation or endorsement: no Apple logos, no "App Store" branding, no "Download on the App Store" badges. Maco is independent and not affiliated with Apple.
- **Don't** use category colours for text, buttons or backgrounds beyond glyph tiles and icon fallbacks.
- **Don't** use orange for anything other than source-available status.
- **Don't** load a web font or a second type family.
- **Don't** add box shadows or outlines to cards, tiles or buttons.
- **Don't** add a manual light/dark toggle; appearance follows the system.
- **Don't** reintroduce the replaced dark Golden Olive showcase or the earlier pastel library.
