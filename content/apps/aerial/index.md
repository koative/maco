Aerial plays the slow-motion aerial footage known from Apple TV as a Mac screen saver. The companion app manages video downloads, playback options, and overlays such as the time or location information.

The project began as a standalone .saver; version 4 moved to this repository as an app with a bundled extension, and the older JohnCoates/Aerial repository now points here. The videos are fetched from their providers, so expect substantial downloads.
