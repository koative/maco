MeetingBar shows the current or next meeting without opening a full calendar. It finds conferencing links in events and provides a quick route into supported meeting services.

Connect a macOS Calendar source or Google Calendar, then choose how titles and countdowns appear. Its filters can reduce clutter from events you do not need to see.
