Ferdium wraps many web services, including chat, email and collaboration tools, into a single window with a sidebar of tabs. Workspaces group services for different contexts, and a built-in to-do panel stays alongside them.

It is a community hard fork of Franz without usage restrictions, and can import existing Franz or Ferdi accounts. Builds are available for Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
