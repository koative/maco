AppPorts helps relocate large applications and selected data directories to an external drive while preserving a familiar way to open them. Its migration approach varies with the application, storage format, and macOS version.

The external drive must remain available for relocated content. Review the readiness checks and migration guidance, and keep a backup before moving important app data.
