Wave Terminal arranges terminals, editors, file previews, web pages and AI chat as blocks in a drag-and-drop layout. SSH connections are a focus: remote sessions reconnect after interruptions, and remote files can be previewed or edited in a built-in editor.

The AI assistant works with your own keys for OpenAI, Anthropic or Google models, or with local models through Ollama and LM Studio, and no account is required. Separate Apple silicon and Intel builds are provided.
