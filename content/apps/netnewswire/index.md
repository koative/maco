NetNewsWire gathers RSS and Atom feeds in a native reader, giving you a chronological place to follow publications and personal sites. Read articles, organize subscriptions and mark items for later.

Use a local account or one of the supported sync services to keep reading in step across devices. Service accounts and their terms are separate from the open-source app.
