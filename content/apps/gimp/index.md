GIMP provides a desktop workspace for editing raster images. Layers, masks, selections, and filters let you combine material and make detailed changes to photographs or graphics.

Use it for image cleanup, composites, and artwork preparation. Official macOS installers are available for supported Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
