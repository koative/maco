Lockpaw places a cover over all connected displays when you press its shortcut, without logging out or sleeping the Mac, so long-running jobs continue in the background. You unlock with the same shortcut, Touch ID, or your account password.

It is aimed at people who leave coding agents and terminal sessions running: the covered screen can light up when a supported agent asks for attention, and alerts can optionally be forwarded to a phone. Keeping the Mac awake with the lid closed is an opt-in feature that relies on a helper you approve separately.
