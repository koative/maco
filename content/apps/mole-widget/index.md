Mole Widget pins a compact system dashboard to the desktop, above the wallpaper and below ordinary windows. It shows CPU load and temperature, memory, disk usage and throughput, network rates, battery health and the busiest processes, styled after the status view of the mole command-line tool, and clicking a section opens Activity Monitor.

The panel appears on every Space, stays out of Mission Control and the app switcher, and remembers its position and width. It requires macOS 14 or later and ships as a signed, notarized DMG that updates itself through Sparkle.
