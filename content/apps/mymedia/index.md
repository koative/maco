MyMedia is a SwiftUI library app for movies and TV shows stored on your own disks, written as an alternative to the TV app for local files. Imported titles are shown with their artwork and details, read from the metadata embedded in each file, so a tagging tool such as Subler is the way to prepare them.

It tracks unwatched movies and episodes, supports collections, pins and favorites, and keeps separate genres for films and series. Playback uses the built-in player or the system's default app. Releases are published as disk images on GitHub.
