Topit pins windows from any app on top of everything else. Pick a window and Topit keeps a live copy floating where you can still move, resize and interact with it, which helps with video calls, reference documents or monitoring.

It uses ScreenCaptureKit and needs Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions; pinning many windows can cost battery. It requires macOS 13 or later. The latest release, 0.1.5, is from December 2024.
