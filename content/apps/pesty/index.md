Pesty slides a row of clipboard cards up from the bottom of the active screen. Search, keyboard navigation, and pinboards make it easy to retrieve recent content or keep frequently reused items.

Optional iCloud synchronization can share history across Macs. Direct downloads are available free, while the optional App Store purchase supports development.
