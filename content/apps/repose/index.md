Repose counts down a work interval in the menu bar and dims the screen when it is time to look away. Work and break lengths are adjustable, and every setting lives in the menu.

Instead of reading your calendar, it pauses while a camera or microphone is active, so calls are not interrupted, and it resets when you step away. The project requires macOS 13 or later.
