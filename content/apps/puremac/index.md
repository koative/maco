PureMac groups several maintenance tasks into one desktop app, including application management, cache inspection, and storage exploration. It presents cleanup choices so you can review what will be affected.

Deleting caches or app data can change application behavior. Keep the review step deliberate and consult the project's explanations of its cleanup boundaries.
