Textream displays a script for streamers, presenters and interviewers. It can highlight words as they are recognised, scroll at a fixed speed, or advance only while you are talking. On the Mac the text appears in a Dynamic Island-style overlay, a draggable window, or full screen on a Sidecar iPad.

The Mac app is available from GitHub releases, Homebrew and the App Store, and an iPhone and iPad companion shares the same listing. The project lists macOS 15 or later as a requirement.
