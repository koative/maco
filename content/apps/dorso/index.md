Dorso observes posture-related input from a camera or supported AirPods and applies a configurable blur when it detects a slouch. The reminder clears as the detected posture changes.

Tracking is processed on the Mac. Different modes require different permissions and hardware; treat it as a configurable reminder rather than a medical assessment.
