WWDC collects Apple's conference keynotes, session videos and other developer video content into one Mac app. It tracks what you have watched, lets you favorite sessions, and shows searchable transcripts that jump to the matching point in a video.

Bookmarks with notes, playback speed controls, picture in picture and optional iCloud sync make it useful for working through a backlog of sessions. It is an independent project, not an Apple product. The most recent GitHub release at review time was 7.5 from June 2024.
