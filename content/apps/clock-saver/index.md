Clock.saver turns the screen saver into a large analogue clock modelled on Braun wristwatches. The screen saver options let you choose among several watch models and their colour variants.

It is a small, finished project: the latest release, 0.7.3, dates from June 2023. The `.saver` bundle is downloaded from GitHub releases or installed with the `clocksaver` Homebrew cask.
