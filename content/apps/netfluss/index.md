NetFluss shows current upload and download speeds in the menu bar and opens a popover with per-adapter details, the busiest apps and a built-in speed test. Bandwidth statistics keep a history of traffic over time, and supported routers such as OPNsense, OpenWRT and UniFi can report whole-network WAN throughput alongside the Mac's own figures.

Later versions grew into a broader network companion, with DNS presets, a connection-path view and a lightweight VPN client for imported OpenVPN, WireGuard and IKEv2 profiles. Mac releases are published as zipped apps on GitHub; a Windows edition is in public beta.
