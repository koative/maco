Device Hub Pro lists Android emulators and phones, iOS simulators and optionally iPhones in one window, with live mirroring, input control and quick toggles for appearance, text size, network, battery, location and language. It also captures screenshots, recordings, logs and crash reports, and installs apps by drag and drop.

Android tooling is installed by the app, while iOS features require Xcode. The project requires macOS 26 on Apple silicon, and notes that some iOS features use private Apple interfaces that can break after macOS or Xcode updates.
