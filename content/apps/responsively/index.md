Responsively App loads a page in several device-sized previews side by side, mirroring scrolling, clicks, and navigation across all of them. It is aimed at checking responsive layouts during web development without resizing a browser window over and over.

Source lives in the main repository, while installers are published in a separate releases repository linked from the official download page.
