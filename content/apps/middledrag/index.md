MiddleDrag translates three-finger gestures into a middle mouse click or drag. This can help with tab management, canvas panning, and applications that expect a physical middle mouse button.

Its menu bar settings expose gesture sensitivity and smoothing. The upstream installer and instructions describe the Accessibility permission it needs.
