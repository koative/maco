Time to Leave keeps a simple record of when each working day starts and ends, including lunch. From your working days and daily hour target it works out when you can leave and notifies you at that point. Days you did not work can be recorded as waivers.

The Electron app also runs on Windows and Linux. Version 4.0.0 (August 2025) is the latest release, with a macOS disk image on GitHub.
