OnlySwitch gathers controls such as desktop-icon visibility, appearance, and keeping the Mac awake. Reorder the switches and add shortcuts to keep common actions close at hand.

The app includes optional features and widgets beyond the basic controls. Choose only the integrations you need and follow the project's setup guidance for them.
