Sol is a keyboard launcher in the Spotlight and Raycast mould, aimed at speed with very little setup. Beyond finding apps it handles custom shortcuts and links, imported browser bookmarks, AppleScript commands, an emoji picker, a scratchpad, quick maths and translation, and your next calendar appointment in the menu bar.

It also includes a clipboard manager, basic window management, and small developer helpers such as UUID generation and JSON formatting. Releases are published on GitHub and the app is available as the official `sol` Homebrew cask.
