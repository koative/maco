Spotifly is a SwiftUI client for browsing your Spotify library, playlists, albums, artists and liked songs, with search, a reorderable queue and a miniplayer. Signing in also registers the Mac as a Spotify Connect device, so it can play locally or control playback on phones and speakers.

Its playback is implemented in Swift following the protocol work of librespot, and it requires Spotify Premium. Current releases need macOS 27 or later and are published as ZIP files on GitHub.
