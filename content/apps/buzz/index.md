Buzz runs OpenAI's Whisper speech-recognition models locally to turn audio and video files, or live microphone input, into text and translations. Transcripts can be reviewed alongside playback and exported as TXT, SRT or VTT subtitles.

Models are downloaded on first use, and processing speed depends heavily on your hardware and the model size you pick. GitHub releases include separate Apple silicon and Intel macOS builds, and a command-line interface is available for scripted jobs.
