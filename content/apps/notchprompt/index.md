Notchprompt keeps a floating script near the top of the display. Adjust its speed and dimensions, import a text file, and use shortcuts to pause, reset, or jump back.

A privacy mode attempts to omit the overlay from capture, but its behavior depends on the recording application. The project currently distributes unsigned builds.
