Sapphire turns the area around the notch on recent MacBooks into a live panel. It shows the currently playing media from any source and the local weather, and can pop up a 20-20-20 eye-break reminder; a beta mixer adjusts per-app and per-device volume and EQ from the same place.

Beyond the notch it bundles a few extra tools: receiving files, photos and links from Android phones through Nearby Share, and a window-snapping system. It is installed from a package on GitHub releases and needs Accessibility and related permissions for its full feature set.
