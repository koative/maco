CaskHub puts a storefront on top of Homebrew's cask catalogue. You can browse curated shelves, categories and install-popularity charts, search across every cask, and install, update or uninstall apps with progress shown in the window instead of a terminal.

It also detects casks you already installed from the command line and can bring apps installed by hand under Homebrew management. If Homebrew is missing, the app walks you through setting it up. Releases are published on GitHub and the app is itself available as a Homebrew cask.
