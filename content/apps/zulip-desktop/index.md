Zulip organises team chat into streams and named topics, so conversations stay threaded instead of scrolling past. The desktop client wraps the Zulip web app and adds what a browser tab lacks: signing in to several organizations side by side, native notifications with inline replies, dock and tray integration, a multi-language spell checker and automatic updates.

It works with Zulip Cloud and self-hosted Zulip servers. Builds for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs are published on GitHub alongside Windows and Linux versions, and the app is also available as a Homebrew cask.
