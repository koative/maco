Itsytv controls an Apple TV on the local network from a small floating panel. It offers a d-pad and the usual remote buttons, maps arrow keys and other keys to remote actions, and lets you type directly into Apple TV text fields.

The panel also shows now-playing artwork and playback controls, an app launcher, and support for pairing several Apple TVs with a global hotkey for each. It is distributed through GitHub releases and Homebrew.
