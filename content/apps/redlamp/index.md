Redlamp concentrates on developing RAW files, with panels that will be familiar to Lightroom users: basic tone and colour, tone curve, colour mixer and grading, detail, lens corrections and crop. Local adjustments use gradient, brush, colour and luminance ranges, and subject, sky and people masks, and it adds healing, focus stacking, and profiles and presets including film simulations.

The project describes itself as alpha software and is changing quickly. Its README states that the Mac app runs on Apple silicon with macOS 26 or later; signed releases are on GitHub, and the project also targets iPad and iPhone.
