Scroll Reverser lets you keep the scrolling direction that feels right for each mouse or trackpad. Its settings distinguish scrolling sources rather than forcing a single preference across the whole Mac.

It runs through macOS event handling and requires Accessibility permission. The official website provides downloads and configuration guidance.
