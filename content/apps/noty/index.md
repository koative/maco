Noty keeps sticky notes out of the way: at rest they are a thin coloured pill on the edge of the display, and moving the pointer there fans them out as labelled tabs. Clicking a tab slides that note open beside the deck, and anything beyond the first few notes waits behind an overflow tab that opens the full list.

There is no Dock icon or main window. Global shortcuts create notes and open a quick-capture box, and a `noty://` URL scheme lets Shortcuts, launchers or a terminal add notes. Notes stay on the Mac; builds for Apple silicon and Intel are published as DMGs on GitHub.
