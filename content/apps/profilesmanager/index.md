ProfilesManager lists the provisioning profiles installed on a Mac in a searchable tree, showing each profile's fields, including the developer certificates it contains. From there you can sort, reveal profiles in Finder, move them to the trash, export a profile or one of its certificates, rename files that are stored under their UUID, and install new profiles by dragging them in.

Dropping an .ipa onto the window shows its embedded profile and Info.plist, and an optional Quick Look plug-in previews .ipa, .xcarchive, .appex and profile files from Finder. The latest release, 2.7, was published in September 2024.
