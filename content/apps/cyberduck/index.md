Cyberduck provides a graphical browser for remote files over protocols such as SFTP and for supported cloud storage services. Bookmarks keep frequently used destinations close at hand.

It is useful when you want to upload, download or inspect remote files without building a command-line workflow. Each storage provider still requires its own account and access permissions.
