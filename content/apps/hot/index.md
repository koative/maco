Hot makes thermal conditions visible while your Mac is under load. It presents temperature information alongside the indicators available for the hardware it is running on.

Intel and Apple Silicon expose different information: Intel builds can show CPU throttling limits, while Apple Silicon builds show thermal pressure and sensor graphs.
