Tiny Image, previously called TinyPNG4Mac, is a third-party client for the TinyPNG compression service. Drag images or folders onto it, or use the Finder Services menu, to compress them and optionally convert to PNG, JPEG, AVIF or WebP.

Compression happens on TinyPNG's servers, so you need your own TinyPNG API key and your images are uploaded to that service. Version 2 requires macOS 13 or later.
