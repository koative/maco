Orkas turns a single chat into a multi-agent workspace: a commander model breaks a goal into steps and hands parts to built-in specialists for research, writing or slide creation. It can also start installed command-line agents such as Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode as local sessions.

Conversations, files, keys and custom agents are stored on your disk, and model calls go directly to the providers you configure with your own API keys. Results depend on the models you pay for and on reviewing what the agents produce.
