Maccy keeps a searchable history of copied items so you can bring an earlier clip back without hunting through documents. Its compact interface works well with keyboard shortcuts.

Pin frequently used items and configure exclusions for apps whose clipboard contents should not be saved. Review these settings before using any clipboard manager with sensitive information.
