IINA+ works alongside the IINA media player to open videos and live streams from Chinese platforms such as Bilibili, Douyu, Huya and Douyin, and adds danmaku, the scrolling viewer comments those sites are known for, on top of playback for supported services. It can also show whether followed live rooms are currently on air.

Support varies by site, and the project's table lists which ones have live status, video decoding and danmaku. Documentation is mainly in Chinese, and IINA itself needs to be installed.
