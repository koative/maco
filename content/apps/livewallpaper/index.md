LiveWallpaper turns video files into a moving desktop background. Drop MP4 or MOV clips into the folder the app opens for you and pick one to loop behind your windows; the lock screen shows a still image rather than the video.

Releases are published on GitHub and through the author's Homebrew tap. The builds are not notarized, so the project documents removing the quarantine attribute before the first launch. The project states a macOS 14 minimum.
