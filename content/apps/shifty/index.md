Shifty adds more direct control over macOS Night Shift, including temperature adjustments and temporary pauses. Exceptions can keep color-sensitive apps from inheriting a warmer display setting.

It relies on Apple's Night Shift support. The project documents supported browsers for website-specific behavior and provides downloadable releases.
