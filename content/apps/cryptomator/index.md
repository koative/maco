Cryptomator presents encrypted vaults as accessible files while they are unlocked. It is useful for adding client-side encryption to folders stored locally or synchronized by another service.

It does not provide cloud storage itself. The desktop app is open source, while mobile distributions and optional offerings may use different purchase arrangements.
