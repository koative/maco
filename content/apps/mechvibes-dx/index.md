MechvibesDX makes every keystroke and click sound like a mechanical keyboard or mouse. Sound packs can be downloaded or made yourself, and audio plays with low latency even while the app is in the background. It is a rewrite of the earlier Mechvibes by the same author.

It runs on Windows, Linux and macOS. The project describes the macOS build as experimental and unsigned, so Gatekeeper must be bypassed on first launch; releases currently provide an Apple silicon disk image.
