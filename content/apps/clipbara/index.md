Clipbara records what you copy and presents it as a card strip along the bottom of the screen, opened with a keyboard shortcut. Clips can be previewed with Quick Look and kept in pinboards for reuse.

The free GitHub DMG and the paid Mac App Store build come from the same source but differ: pasting directly into the active app and the clip queue are DMG-only, while iCloud sync and the iPhone app require the App Store version.
