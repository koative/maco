AutoDoc records the screen and audio of a meeting, transcribes it on the device, and produces notes you can edit afterwards. Clicking a timestamp in the notes jumps the recording back to that moment, and an Ask AI panel answers questions across past meetings. Summaries run through a local Ollama instance that the app manages, so no API keys or cloud AI service are involved.

It connects to Google and Microsoft calendars, detects meetings, and can start recording automatically for selected events. The project is published by the Duet Display team and ships installers for macOS and Windows; supported languages depend on the machine, and 8 GB Macs are limited to English.
