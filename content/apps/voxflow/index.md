VoxFlow is a voice keyboard first: hold a key, speak, and the text is inserted into whatever field has focus. Recognition starts with Apple's built-in speech engine, and local or cloud providers can be configured, with personal correction rules aimed at mixed Chinese and English technical vocabulary. Around that sits a workbench that keeps dictations, screenshots, screen recordings, clipboard items and notes as searchable local records, with OCR, translation and summaries for captured images.

It can also pass spoken instructions to registered coding-agent sessions such as Codex or Claude, and its launcher can query a configured language model. The macOS edition, which the project says needs macOS 15 or later, is the most complete; DMGs are published on GitHub releases alongside Windows and iOS builds.
