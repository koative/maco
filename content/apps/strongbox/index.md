Strongbox opens KeePass (KDB and KDBX) and Password Safe 3 databases on the Mac, iPhone and iPad. Databases can be stored locally or in cloud storage, YubiKey is supported, and entries can hold file attachments.

The app is distributed through the App Store with a free tier and paid Pro features. The repository publishes the functional source for transparency, but omits artwork and some build configuration, and the developers do not accept contributions.
