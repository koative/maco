PicGo sends screenshots and image files to a configured hosting service and puts the resulting link on the clipboard in Markdown, HTML, plain URL or a custom format. Uploads can start from drag and drop, the clipboard, a global shortcut or the Finder context menu.

GitHub, Imgur and several cloud storage vendors are supported directly, and plugins add others such as S3-compatible storage. Editors including Typora, Obsidian and VS Code can hand their images to it, natively or through plugins. You need your own account and credentials for whichever host you choose.
