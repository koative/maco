Kaset wraps YouTube Music and YouTube in a Mac app with a sidebar library, search, queue management, synced lyrics where available and podcasts. It reports what is playing to Control Center, responds to media keys and keeps audio playing after the window closes.

Playback runs through WebKit, and protected YouTube Music content requires your own Premium subscription. Some features, including on-device AI helpers, need macOS 26. It is not affiliated with Google.
