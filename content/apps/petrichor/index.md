Petrichor plays music files from folders you point it at, organising them into albums, artists and other library views. Smart playlists, a drag-and-drop queue, folder browsing, synced lyrics and saved internet radio stations cover everyday listening without a streaming subscription.

It integrates with the menu bar, Dock and Shortcuts, and offers gapless playback, crossfade, ReplayGain and Last.fm scrobbling. Library views depend on well-tagged files, so messy metadata produces messy results.
