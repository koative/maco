Doughnut subscribes to podcast feeds and plays episodes in a classic three-column layout. Its library is stored as files that can sit on an internal or shared network drive, and it can hold favourites and podcasts created without a feed for one-off downloads.

The most recent release is from 2022 and the repository has been quiet since, so expect a stable but unmaintained app.
