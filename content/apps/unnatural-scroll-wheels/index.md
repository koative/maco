UnnaturalScrollWheels separates the scrolling behavior of a conventional mouse wheel from your trackpad. It also exposes settings for scroll acceleration and wheel response.

The app needs Accessibility permission to intercept and adjust scroll events. Download the disk image from the upstream releases page.
