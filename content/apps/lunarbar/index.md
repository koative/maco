LunarBar puts a small month view in the menu bar with the Chinese lunar calendar alongside Gregorian dates. It also marks public holidays and can show events and reminders from the system calendars.

The app is deliberately minimal, sandboxed, signed and notarized, and its interface and documentation are mainly in Chinese. Current releases target macOS 26, with older builds kept for macOS 13 and 14; it is also available as a Homebrew cask.
