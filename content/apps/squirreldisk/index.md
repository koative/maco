SquirrelDisk scans a drive or folder and draws the result as an animated sunburst or treemap, making it easy to see which directories take up space. From there you can review and remove what you no longer need.

Version 2 is a rewrite by the original author and can also scan servers over SSH and some cloud storage. The macOS build is distributed outside the App Store, so the first launch needs confirmation in Privacy & Security settings.
