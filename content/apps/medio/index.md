Medio compares two pieces of text and refreshes the highlighted differences live while either one is edited, marking changes down to individual words. It has light and dark modes.

It is a compact SwiftUI and AppKit app from nuance-dev; since version 3.0 the comparison relies on the Differ Swift package. The latest GitHub release, 3.2.0 from November 2024, ships as a zipped app, and the author describes it as an early project that may still have rough edges.
