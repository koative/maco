PeaZip is a full archive manager with a file-browser interface. It extracts a long list of formats, creates archives such as 7Z, ZIP, TAR, and Zstandard, and supports encryption, split volumes, and integrity checks.

It is a cross-platform application rather than a Mac-specific utility, so its interface follows its own conventions. Separate Apple silicon and Intel DMGs are published on GitHub and linked from the official macOS page.
