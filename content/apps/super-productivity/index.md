Super Productivity combines a task list with planning and time tracking. Tasks can be split into sub-tasks, grouped by project and tag, laid out on boards or a daily schedule next to calendar events, and timed for timesheets and work summaries. Break reminders and Pomodoro or countdown focus sessions are built in.

It can pull issues from Jira, GitHub, GitLab, OpenProject and several other services. The Mac app is distributed on GitHub releases and the App Store, alongside web, Windows, Linux and Android versions.
