MacsyZones brings the FancyZones idea to the Mac: draw a layout of zones, including overlapping or grid-based ones, then drop windows into a zone to resize them to fit. Layouts can differ per display and be switched with shortcuts.

The app is free to download from GitHub releases or the official site. A paid “Pro” licence is offered as a way to support the developer; according to the website it changes only the app's name, not its features.
