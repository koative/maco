OpenClip shows a compact bar of actions next to text you select, in the spirit of PopClip. Built-in actions change case, search, copy, and evaluate math, and a hotkey opens a searchable palette of every action.

Extensions can be written in JavaScript, shell, AppleScript, or as URL schemes, and optional AI actions can use local Ollama models or your own provider keys. It needs Accessibility permission to read the current selection.
