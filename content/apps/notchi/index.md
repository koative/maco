Notchi turns the notch area into a quick text field. A line can become an Apple Note, a Reminders entry when it contains a time, a question for an AI model, or a larger task passed to a coding-agent CLI working in a chosen folder. A hardware notch is optional.

The app is free; AI answers use either Notchi's own prepaid models or your own provider key, local endpoint or signed-in agent CLI. Releases are currently not notarized, so macOS asks for approval on first launch.
