Snk is a compact take on Snake from the developer of Itsycal. Pick one of three levels with the number keys, steer with the arrow keys or WASD, and start again after a crash with a single key press.

The current version is a notarized app for macOS 11 and later that the developer describes as running natively on Intel and Apple silicon; an older build remains available for macOS 10.10. Downloads come from mowglii.com, and the app updates itself through Sparkle. Its soundtrack uses third-party tracks credited in the README.
