AirBattery collects battery readings from nearby Apple devices and Bluetooth peripherals and shows them on your Mac. Levels can appear in a Dock menu, the menu bar, or desktop widgets, and devices can be hidden individually.

iPhones and iPads need to trust the Mac and share its network for discovery, and a LAN feature can read batteries from other Macs. The latest release is 1.6.3 from June 2025.
