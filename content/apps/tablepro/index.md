TablePro connects to relational and NoSQL databases through bundled and installable drivers, and combines a SQL editor with autocomplete and a Vim mode, an inline-editable data grid, tabs and split panes. Connections can go through SSH tunnels or TLS, and query history is searchable.

Optional AI features work with a key for your chosen provider or a local Ollama model. The core client is free for every supported database, while some extras in the Mac app require a paid license. The source is published under AGPL-3.0.
