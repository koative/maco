Thunderbird combines email, calendars, and contacts in a desktop workspace. It supports multiple accounts and extensions for adjusting how messages and tasks are handled.

The application is distinct from any email hosting provider. Its current source lives in the Thunderbird desktop repository and builds on Mozilla's shared platform.
