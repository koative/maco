AutoRaise brings a window forward after the pointer rests over it for a configurable interval. It can also reposition the pointer when switching applications with the keyboard.

The downloadable app includes a menu bar interface; that interface has its own upstream repository. AutoRaise works with separate macOS windows, not panes inside an application's window.
