TaskExplorer brings several views of a running process into one interface: signing information, loaded libraries, files, and connections. It helps investigate activity that a simple process list cannot explain.

The project documents its system-extension permissions and optional integrations. Some enrichment features, such as VirusTotal lookups, require separate setup.
