Marker Data reads markers, and optionally captions, from Final Cut Pro timelines and turns them into structured exports such as CSV, TSV, Excel, YouTube chapter lists or JSON for Notion and Airtable. It can render a still or animated GIF at each marker, burn in labels and pick out colour swatches for shot reference.

It plugs into Final Cut Pro through a share destination and workflow extension, and saved configurations make repeat exports consistent. It is only useful alongside Final Cut Pro.
