Pasteboard Viewer lists the items on the general, find, drag and other system pasteboards and shows the raw data stored under each type identifier. It is aimed at developers checking what their own app writes on copy or drag, rather than at people who want a clipboard history.

The current version is distributed through the Mac App Store and needs a recent macOS release; the project README links older builds for earlier systems and a yearly non-App Store download.
