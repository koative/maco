Wallnetic sets a video as the desktop background. It ships no wallpaper library of its own: you drag in an MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM or GIF file, or find clips on one of the wallpaper sites it can browse from inside the app, and each display can play something different.

The app needs macOS 13 or later, has no account, subscription or analytics, and is available both from GitHub releases and the Mac App Store. It does not run Wallpaper Engine scene files, only ordinary video.
