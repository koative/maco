Pastel searches the App Store for iOS, iPadOS and visionOS apps, shows their version history, and fetches a chosen build as an IPA from Apple with the signed-in Apple account. Free apps can be added to the account first; paid ones must already have been bought. Downloaded IPAs are kept in a library grouped by app, from which they can be revealed in Finder or sent to a nearby iPhone or iPad with AirDrop.

The interface is in Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Thai, and the README is written in Chinese. The project states that it needs macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon; DMGs are published on the GitHub releases page.
