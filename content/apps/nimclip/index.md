Nimclip keeps a history of copied text, links, code, rich text and images in the menu bar, opened with a global shortcut. Items can be filtered by type, favourited, given coloured tags, or merged into one paste, and images are indexed with on-device text recognition so you can search for words inside them.

History size and retention are configurable, and favourites do not count toward the limit. The interface is available in Simplified Chinese and English; release builds are provided separately for Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
