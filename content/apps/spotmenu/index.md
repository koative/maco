SpotMenu puts the artist and title of the playing track in the menu bar and adds a hover overlay with album art, play/pause and skip controls. It works with Spotify and Apple Music, detecting the active player automatically or letting you pick one.

Global shortcuts and a compact display mode are built in. Liking tracks in Spotify is optional and requires creating your own Spotify developer app and pasting its client ID into the settings.
