Reminders MenuBar brings the reminders stored in Apple's Reminders system into a compact menu. Search tasks, filter lists, adjust due dates, or mark an item complete without opening the full Reminders app.

It needs permission to access your reminders. Changes use the same underlying reminder store, including accounts synchronized through macOS.
