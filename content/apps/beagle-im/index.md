Beagle IM connects to any standard XMPP (Jabber) server, so you can chat with contacts across providers rather than inside a single company's network. It handles one-to-one and group chats, file transfer, and audio and video calls, and conversations can be protected with OMEMO end-to-end encryption.

It is developed by Tigase on top of its TigaseSwift XMPP library. Builds are published on GitHub releases and through Tigase's own Homebrew tap.
