LimeChat is a straightforward IRC client that gathers several servers and their channels into a single window and leans on keyboard shortcuts for navigation. It is also the project Textual originally forked from.

It is distributed free on the Mac App Store. Its last App Store update was in June 2020 and the repository has been quiet since 2021, so expect a dated client without newer IRCv3 features.
