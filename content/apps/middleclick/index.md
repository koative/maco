MiddleClick translates multi-finger clicks or taps into a middle mouse click. That can open links in background tabs, close tabs, and trigger other middle-button actions in applications.

The original project is now considered finished and points to a separate successor, WheelClick. This entry covers the still-available GPL-licensed MiddleClick application.
