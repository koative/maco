Yippy adds a visual history to the Mac clipboard. Open the panel with a shortcut and retrieve an earlier item after something else has replaced the current clipboard.

Its preferences include a history limit and launch-at-login behavior. The upstream releases provide a direct macOS download. Its latest published release is from 2021, so check compatibility with your macOS version.
