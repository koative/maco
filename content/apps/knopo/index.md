Knopo follows the Logseq style of note-taking: every line is a block that can be indented, folded, zoomed into, referenced or embedded elsewhere. Pages link with double brackets, tags and live queries gather related blocks, and a daily journal acts as the starting page.

The notes stay as readable Markdown files in a folder you choose, with the app maintaining a search index alongside. It is an early 0.x release, and the published builds at review time were for Apple silicon only.
