ScrollSnap places an adjustable selection rectangle over the screen; you start the capture, scroll the content inside it, and the app joins the frames into a single long image. A floating menu and thumbnail handle saving to the Desktop, a folder, the clipboard or Preview.

The free compiled app is published as a ZIP on GitHub releases, and the developer also lists it on the Mac App Store for managed updates. The interface is translated into several languages.
