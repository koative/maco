TetherShot takes full-resolution screenshots of connected phones from the Mac. A trusted iPhone can be captured over a cable or over Wi-Fi, and Android phones are reached through ADB. Each capture is saved to a folder you choose and copied to the clipboard, and a global hotkey can grab every connected device at once.

It is useful for app developers and anyone writing documentation. Wi-Fi capture of iPhones goes through a developer-services tunnel provided by pymobiledevice3, and Android capture requires debugging access through ADB.
