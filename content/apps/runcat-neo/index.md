RunCat Neo puts an animated runner in the menu bar: a cat by default, with other built-in runners such as a dog, a coffee cup and a water drop, and community-made runners shared through the project's Runner Gallery.

Its dashboard shows system readings and can render extra cards from any local JSON file in a documented format, with samples for Claude Code and Codex usage. The app is distributed through the Mac App Store and requires macOS 26 or later.
