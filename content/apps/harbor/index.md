Harbor handles direct HTTP and HTTPS downloads, public media links with format selection, magnet links and `.torrent` files in one window. Torrents can be previewed to pick files, watch folders and seeding limits are supported, and batches of links can be queued together.

Speed can be capped globally or per download through traffic profiles, and finished files open in Quick Look. It works locally with no account or hosted backend.
