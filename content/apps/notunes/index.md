noTunes intercepts attempts to launch Apple Music or iTunes, such as those triggered by reconnecting headphones. A menu bar control lets you enable or disable the behavior.

You can configure a replacement application or website for those launch attempts. The upstream instructions explain startup behavior and how to restore normal launches.
