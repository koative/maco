TypeCue replays a script you wrote in advance. Each press of a global hotkey sends the next block to whatever field has focus as ordinary keystrokes at a human-like pace, which keeps live demos and recorded tutorials free of typos and identical between takes.

Scripts are managed from the menu bar, with an optional floating panel. The app needs Accessibility permission to send keystrokes and, per its README, macOS 14 or later. It is a young, small project.
