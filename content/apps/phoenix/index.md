Phoenix exposes windows, screens, spaces, apps, the mouse and system events to a JavaScript configuration file. You write the behaviour you want, such as tiling layouts, keyboard shortcuts or event-driven rules, and Phoenix runs it with a small footprint. Languages that compile to JavaScript, such as TypeScript, also work.

Its behaviour comes from your own configuration, so it suits people comfortable writing some JavaScript. Phoenix needs Accessibility permission. The latest release, 4.0.1, dates from mid-2024 and is also available as a Homebrew cask.
