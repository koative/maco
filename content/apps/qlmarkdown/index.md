QLMarkdown adds a Quick Look extension for Markdown and related text formats. Its settings control how previews render, including themes and optional support for diagrams and mathematical expressions.

It is a preview extension with a configuration app, not a standalone writing editor. The upstream guide explains how to enable the extension after installation.
