OpenInTerminal adds a Finder toolbar button and menu bar item that open the current folder or selection in a terminal such as Terminal, iTerm2, Ghostty or kitty, or in an editor such as VS Code, Sublime Text or Xcode. Custom apps are supported, and the full version adds a preferences window and keyboard shortcuts.

Two minimal companions, OpenInTerminal-Lite and OpenInEditor-Lite, skip the preferences window and do one job each from the Finder toolbar. They are published on GitHub releases, and the main app and OpenInTerminal-Lite are also Homebrew casks.
