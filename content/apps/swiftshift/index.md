SwiftShift brings window movement and resizing to gestures that work across a window's surface. Its quadrant-based resizing and app exclusions let you tune the behavior to your usual desktop layout.

Downloads are available from the project's releases, alongside a pay-what-you-want purchase option. The app needs macOS Accessibility permission to manipulate windows.
