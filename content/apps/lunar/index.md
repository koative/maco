Lunar brings external-display controls into macOS, including hardware brightness on compatible DDC/CI monitors. It also offers adaptive behavior and shortcuts for multi-monitor setups.

The published source uses the MIT license, while the official app offers paid features. Check the feature comparison and monitor compatibility before choosing a download or purchase.
