Usage4Claude reads the usage limits of a Claude or Codex subscription and shows them in the menu bar, with colour-coded icons per limit and a detail window that charts five-hour, weekly and per-model allowances against their reset times.

It needs you to sign in through the browser, or paste a Claude session key, so it can query your account; free Claude accounts have no usage data to show. It is an independent project, not affiliated with Anthropic or OpenAI.
