MountMate lists connected external drives and their mounted state, with controls for mounting, unmounting, and checking free space. It also supports automatic reconnection for configured network shares.

This keeps occasional backup drives and remote volumes within reach. Finish file operations before unmounting a volume or using its force-eject option.
