Telegram Desktop provides access to Telegram chats, channels, and shared files from a computer. The cross-platform client publishes its source under GPL.

This is the Qt-based Telegram Desktop app, distinct from the separate native Telegram for macOS client. Standard cloud chats should not be confused with end-to-end encrypted secret chats.
