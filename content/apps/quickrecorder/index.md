QuickRecorder offers several ways to capture your Mac, from an individual window to an entire display. It includes system-audio capture and presentation features such as pointer emphasis and a camera overlay.

Some recording options depend on the macOS version. Its settings can keep microphone and system audio in separate tracks for later editing.
