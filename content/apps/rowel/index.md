Rowel keeps passwords, passkeys, payment cards, identity documents, SSH keys and developer secrets in an encrypted vault on your own devices, without an account or subscription. Sync is optional and goes through a cloud storage account you control, which only receives ciphertext.

It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux, with an iOS app in beta and a browser extension. The project was formerly known as Swifty, and the desktop app updates itself from signed releases.
