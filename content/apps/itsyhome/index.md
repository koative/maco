Itsyhome puts your smart home in a menu: rooms, devices, scenes and custom groups, with favourites at the top and similar devices folded into one-click groups. It works with Apple HomeKit and with Home Assistant through its WebSocket API, and individual items can be pinned to their own menu bar icons.

Distribution is through the Mac App Store and the developer's Homebrew tap. Some features, such as iCloud sync, deeplinks and cameras, are marked as Pro in the project's feature list.
