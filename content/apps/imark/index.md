Imark opens Markdown files as rendered documents and keeps them current while you edit them in another app, holding your scroll position across saves. It adds a foldable outline, wiki-link navigation, and a Quick Look extension so Finder previews show formatted text.

Comments attached to a passage are written into the document as HTML comments, which keeps them portable. An editing switch is available, but the app is designed first as a reader alongside your usual editor.
