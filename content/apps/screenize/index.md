Screenize is a screen recorder for product demos and tutorials in the style of Screen Studio. It tracks your pointer and clicks while recording, then generates zooms and pans automatically; every generated keyframe sits on a multi-track timeline where it can be adjusted, alongside click ripples and keystroke overlays.

The author paused development in May 2026, noting that newer open-source alternatives have appeared, and the most recent release is v0.4.3 from March 2026. The code remains open and disk images are still available on GitHub releases.
