MiaoYan arranges folders, notes and the editor in three columns over a directory of Markdown files, which can live in iCloud Drive or another synced folder. It supports wikilinks, LaTeX, Mermaid diagrams, version history, formatting cleanup and turning a note into a presentation.

The GitHub releases and Homebrew cask provide the free open-source Mac app. A paid App Store edition adds iPhone and iPad versions.
