Syncthing for macOS packages the Syncthing service with a Mac application wrapper. It helps keep the service running and provides convenient access to its synchronization controls.

The wrapper is MIT licensed; the bundled Syncthing engine is separately MPL-2.0 licensed. It synchronizes configured folders between devices rather than providing cloud storage.
