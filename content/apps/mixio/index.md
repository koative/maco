Mixio adds a menu bar window that lists apps producing sound and gives each its own slider, including individual browser tabs. A 10-band equalizer and an optional custom volume overlay complete it, styled to resemble the system's own menus.

It requires macOS 26 or later and System Audio Recording permission; Accessibility access is needed only for the custom overlay. Signed DMGs are on the releases page.
