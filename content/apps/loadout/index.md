Loadout collects everything installed into the coding assistants on a Mac, such as personal and project skills, plugin contents, slash commands, subagents and MCP servers from Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity, into one inventory. It reads the assistants' own session logs to show how often each item actually ran and when, and marks counts it cannot verify instead of reporting zero.

Items can be switched off, copied to another assistant, or edited when you own them, and a project view shows what an assistant will see in a given folder. It requires macOS 15 or later and is distributed as a signed DMG and through the developer's Homebrew tap.
