Tungsten Edge adds a Windows-style taskbar where every open window gets its own card, so returning to a specific document is one click instead of a trip through the Dock's window list. It adds an app drawer, pinned folders that open like Dock stacks, multi-display support and optional auto-hide.

Ready-to-run builds come from the official website rather than GitHub releases. The app needs Accessibility permission to list and switch windows.
