Dimble sorts menu bar icons into three groups: always visible, hidden until you hover, click or press a shortcut, and always hidden unless you explicitly ask for them. A layout editor shows the bar as a drawing you rearrange and then apply in one pass, or you can Command-drag icons across the divider directly in the menu bar.

It is written for the menu bar Apple rebuilt in macOS 27 and requires that release; the project credits Thaw and Ice for the groundwork on managing it. The repository was previously named Pelmet. Builds with in-app updates and an optional beta channel are published on GitHub.
