Radiola plays internet radio streams from a menu bar menu, which is usually all you need to pick a station and listen. A separate station window manages the list and works as a fuller player, and a history view shows what has been playing.

It is meant as a small alternative to running a full music app just for streams. Disk images are published on GitHub releases and through Homebrew.
