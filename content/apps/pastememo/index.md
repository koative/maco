PasteMemo keeps a searchable history of what you copy and recognises the kind of content in each clip: links get page previews, code is highlighted, colours show swatches, and files, images and phone numbers get their own actions. Clips can be pinned, filtered by type or source app, and expired after a chosen number of days.

A global shortcut opens a quick paste panel. Beyond pasting text, a clip can be dropped as a file, pasted as a path or filename, or pasted followed by Return for terminal use. It requires macOS 14 or later, with separate Apple silicon and Intel DMGs on GitHub.
