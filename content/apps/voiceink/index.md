VoiceInk records while you hold or toggle a shortcut and types the transcription into the current app. Speech recognition runs with local models, and a personal dictionary handles names and jargon. Modes can change formatting and optional AI post-processing depending on the app or website in front.

The source is GPL-3.0, while the official signed download starts with a trial and asks for a purchased license to keep using it. The repository's license file is an abbreviated GPLv3 notice that points to the full text. The project lists macOS 15 or later.
