NeoHtop is a graphical system monitor with process filtering, sortable resource columns, and pinned processes. It helps you focus on a particular application or group of background tasks.

This entry covers the desktop application, not the separate CLI. The release downloads include builds for Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.
