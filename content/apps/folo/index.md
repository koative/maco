Folo collects blogs, news sites, newsletters, podcasts, video channels and social feeds in one timeline. Its optional AI features can summarise, translate or answer questions about what you follow, and subscriptions and read state sync across web, mobile and desktop.

Desktop builds for macOS are published on GitHub releases and the project also links a Mac App Store version. The repository is AGPL-3.0, with a stated exception that the bundled MingCute icon set in `icons/mgc` may not be redistributed.
