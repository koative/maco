SaneSales is aimed at independent sellers who use more than one storefront. It connects to Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad and Stripe with your own API keys and combines their revenue, orders, products and refunds into one dashboard, with charts, search, CSV export and today's total in the menu bar.

Data is fetched directly from those providers and kept on your devices; keys can sync through iCloud Keychain. The Mac app is a free download from the official website and GitHub releases, and the same developer also offers iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch versions.
