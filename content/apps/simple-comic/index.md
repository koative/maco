Simple Comic is a comic viewer designed to keep page turning and zooming out of the way. It handles two-page spreads, rotation and reading order, and bundles a Quick Look extension for previews and thumbnails of comic archives. On Apple silicon it can make text in pages selectable for copying or speech.

This repository is a maintained fork of the original project, which was abandoned by its first developer. Builds are published as GitHub releases.
