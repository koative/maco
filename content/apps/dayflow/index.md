Dayflow records lightweight screen captures through the day and has an AI model summarise them into activity cards on a timeline. From that record it drafts standup notes, weekly reviews of focus and distractions, and Markdown exports, and it can answer questions about past work in a chat panel.

Recordings and the database stay on the Mac. Analysis can run through local models or the user's own Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude access; the developer also sells an optional managed-AI subscription. The free app ships as a disk image on GitHub releases.
