MiniSim lists installed iOS simulators and Android emulators in the menu bar and starts them with one click or a shortcut. It also copies device names and identifiers, deletes simulators, cold-boots Android devices or launches them without audio, and marks which ones are running.

It is useful for mobile developers who switch devices often, and pairs with an optional Raycast extension. It works with the simulators and emulators already set up through Xcode and the Android SDK.
