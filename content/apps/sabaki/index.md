Sabaki is a board for the game of Go that doubles as a game-record editor. It opens and saves SGF files and collections, imports a couple of other server formats, and lets you mark up positions with arrows and lines, write comments, branch into variations and move through a fast game tree.

Engines that speak the Go Text Protocol can be attached to play against or to analyse positions, with analysis overlays for engines that support them. Scoring, a score estimator, guess mode, autoplay and themeable board textures are included. Releases cover macOS on Apple silicon and Intel as well as Windows and Linux.
