Calendr adds a calendar to the menu bar that opens to a month view with your upcoming events. New events can be typed as a single line, such as a title followed by "next Friday from 10 to 12", and the app fills in the date, time, duration and target calendar while you type; English and Czech phrasing are understood.

Less obvious features include extra time zones in the menu bar item, set through its date format, and a `calendr://` URL scheme for jumping to a date. Releases are published on GitHub as a zipped app, and the author also maintains a Homebrew tap.
