stts keeps a list of the online services you rely on, from cloud providers to developer platforms, and shows their current state behind a single menu bar icon. One click shows the list, another opens a service's own status page, and optional notifications report outages and recoveries.

The app is distributed through the Mac App Store. The repository holds the MIT-licensed source along with a script contributors use to add new services from their public status pages.
