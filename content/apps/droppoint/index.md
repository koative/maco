DropPoint holds dragged content while you navigate to its destination. It is useful when the source and destination are in different full-screen windows or virtual desktops.

Drop content onto the shelf, switch to the target, and drag it onward. The upstream releases include separate macOS downloads alongside Windows and Linux builds.
