Tuta Mail, formerly Tutanota, provides encrypted email, contacts and calendar, and its desktop app brings the same client to the Mac. Mail between Tuta users is end-to-end encrypted, and messages to other providers can be protected with a password.

You need a Tuta account to use it, with free and paid plans offered by the service. The client source for web, desktop and mobile lives in one GPL-licensed repository, and macOS disk images are attached to desktop releases on GitHub.
