QuickNetStats sits in the menu bar and updates as soon as the Mac gains or loses its connection, rather than waiting for macOS to notice. It lets you read and copy your IPv4 addresses at a glance and opens a panel with more detail about the current connection.

It also gives an estimate of link quality without running a speed test. The app supports macOS 13 and later, but the developer notes that the network quality reading relies on an API available only from macOS 26.
