BongoCat puts an animated cat in a small window on your desktop. Its eyes and paws follow the pointer, and it plays a matching motion whenever you press a key, a mouse button or a gamepad button. The window can be moved anywhere, and you can load your own Live2D models or pick community ones from the project's companion model list.

It is purely for fun and works offline. The project builds for macOS and Windows, and its GitHub releases include separate Apple silicon and Intel DMGs.
