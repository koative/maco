Snapzy is a menu-bar capture tool covering full-screen, area and window screenshots, screen recording, annotation and basic editing in one place. It positions itself as an open-source alternative to commercial capture apps such as CleanShot X.

Builds are signed and notarized and require macOS 13 or later; recent GitHub releases are labelled beta. The app is not sandboxed, and screen capture needs the usual macOS recording permission.
