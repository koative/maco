Comfy Desktop installs and manages ComfyUI environments from a desktop application. It provides a route into node-based workflows for supported generative models and tools, and can keep several independent setups side by side.

The macOS build targets supported Apple Silicon systems. The app is available under AGPL-3.0-or-later, with a separate commercial license offered by Comfy Org. Models and custom nodes have their own licenses, hardware requirements, and installation needs.
