Mac Sai bundles the kinds of tools found in commercial Mac cleaners: scans for system junk, caches, mail attachments and build folders, a large-file finder, app uninstallation, and performance and protection modules, plus a menu bar widget.

It is a young project that moves quickly, so review every scan result before deleting; the app sends removals to the Trash first. Upstream says it collects no telemetry. Its malware checks are not a substitute for dedicated security tools.
