LaunchNow brings back a grid of app icons for people who miss Launchpad on macOS Sequoia and later. It can run full screen in the classic style or as a smaller window, and lets you drag icons into folders, rearrange them, hide apps you never open and search across everything, including folder contents.

Pages flip with the keyboard or a configurable four-finger trackpad gesture, and layouts can be exported and restored. The app runs in the background; releases are distributed as ZIP archives on GitHub.
