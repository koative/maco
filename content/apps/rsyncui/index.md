RsyncUI turns rsync configuration into reusable tasks with a graphical interface. It helps manage parameters, estimate transfers, and inspect results without assembling every command manually.

The transfer behavior still depends on the selected rsync options and destinations. Review estimates and deletion settings before running a synchronization task.
