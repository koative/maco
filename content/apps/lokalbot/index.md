LokalBot records microphone and meeting-app audio without adding a bot to the call, then produces transcripts, recaps, decisions, and action items that link back to the conversation. One search spans those transcripts and, if you enable it, a record of what appeared on screen, by keyword or by meaning. Dictation and text autocomplete also run on the Mac.

Models download on first use, after which the built-in features can work offline; Ollama, OpenAI-compatible servers, and Apple Intelligence are optional alternatives. A bundled command-line tool and MCP server can give agents read-only access to the meeting library when you allow it. LokalBot requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 15 or later and is distributed as a DMG or through the project's Homebrew tap.
