Coluracetam focuses on reading Markdown rather than writing long documents. Pressing the spacebar on a `.md` file in Finder shows the same rendered preview the app uses, with thumbnails generated for Finder as well. Inside the app, a split view puts the source beside the live preview, and selection and search work across the rendered document.

Light and dark previews, zoom shortcuts and PDF or HTML export cover the rest. If another editor changes the file, the app reloads it without discarding unsaved edits. It requires macOS 26 and is available as a DMG from GitHub, through the author's Homebrew tap, or on the Mac App Store.
