OBS Studio combines video, screen captures, audio and other sources into scenes for recording or live streaming. Use a simple capture setup or build a more involved production.

Audio mixing, transitions and extensibility make it adaptable to demonstrations and broadcasts. Check the official macOS installation guidance and capture permissions before recording.
